# App Review Appeal — Guideline 4.2 (Minimum Functionality)

Use this in App Store Connect → Resolution Center. Attaching a short screen
recording that demonstrates the features below is strongly recommended.

## Reply to send

Thank you for the review. We respectfully believe News Sphere offers substantial
native functionality beyond a web-browsing experience, and we'd like to clarify
the on-device features, several of which may not be obvious on a first pass:

- Age-appropriate curation: the user enters an age and the app natively restricts
  which news categories appear, providing a safer experience for younger readers.
- Personalized recommendations computed on-device from the user's reading history
  ("Suggested for you").
- Reading streaks and progress: the app tracks a daily reading streak, longest
  streak, and total articles read on-device, and surfaces them in the app and in a
  Home Screen widget (WidgetKit).
- Accessibility: built-in text-to-speech read-aloud with selectable, language-
  matched voices.
- A fully localized interface across 46 languages, including right-to-left layouts.
- "News about a country in your language" — original functionality that reframes
  coverage by country and locale.
- Native aggregation that de-duplicates, sorts, cleans, and attributes stories from
  many independent publishers into one consistent interface, with offline caching,
  on-device bookmarking, and search.

The in-app web view is only an optional "Read full story" action, not the core
experience. We're happy to provide a demo video walking through these features, and
we welcome specific guidance on any area you'd like us to strengthen.

## Unique value-add points (talking points)

1. Age-based content curation — native per-age category gating (Kids / Teens / Adults).
2. On-device personalized "Suggested for you" recommendation engine (categories + keywords, recency-weighted).
3. Reading streaks / progress tracking, shown in-app and via a Home Screen widget.
4. Read-aloud text-to-speech with selectable, language-matched natural voices (accessibility).
5. Fully localized UI across 46 languages, including RTL layouts.
6. "News about any country, in your language" (~70 countries, locale-aware).
7. Native aggregation & transformation: de-duplication, recency sorting, headline cleanup, source attribution, topic tabs.
8. Offline caching — opens instantly and works with no connection.
9. On-device bookmarking, multi-source search, privacy model with no accounts/ads/tracking.
10. Native app behaviors: universal iPhone/iPad, adaptive layout, portrait + landscape, pull-to-refresh, widget.

## Tips

- Make the differentiators visible to the reviewer: Suggestions appear after reading a
  few articles; read-aloud/voice must be enabled; the streak and widget are new. A short
  screen recording is the most effective way to clear a 4.2 rejection.
- If pushed again, add further native depth (e.g., daily digest, topic-following with
  local notifications, offline read-later with saved full text).
