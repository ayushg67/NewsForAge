# Newsphere — App Review Answers

(Paste into App Store Connect "Review Notes." Under 4000 characters.)

1. DEVICES & OS TESTED
Tested on iPhone 17 Pro (iOS 26.4 and 26.5) and iPad Pro 11-inch M5 (iPadOS 26.5). The app is universal (iPhone + iPad), supports portrait and landscape, and deploys to iOS 17.0 and later.

2. PURPOSE & TARGET AUDIENCE
Newsphere is a worldwide news reader that tailors content to the reader's age. Problem it solves: general news apps expose everyone — including children — to the same unfiltered topics. Value: you enter an age once, and the app limits which news categories appear (younger readers see friendly topics like science, sports, animals and space; teens add world/tech/environment; adults get full coverage including politics, business and crime). It also offers 46 languages, an optional country focus, text-to-speech read-aloud, and personalized suggestions. Target audience: general audience and families, including younger readers and multilingual users who want news from a chosen country in their own language.

3. SETUP & ACCESS
No account, login, or credentials are required, and no sample files are needed. On first launch, enter an age and tap "Show me the news." Main features:
- News tab: topic tabs across the top; tap any article to read a summary; "Read full story" opens the source; "Listen" reads it aloud.
- Search tab: search worldwide news.
- Saved tab: bookmarked articles.
- Settings tab: change age, pick language, focus on a country, and choose/preview the read-aloud voice.
For the most natural voices, an Enhanced/Premium voice can be downloaded in iOS Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices (optional).

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES USED
Newsphere displays public RSS feeds from established news publishers and does not use any authentication, payment, analytics, tracking, or AI services. Data providers:
- BBC and BBC World Service, The Guardian, El País (Spain), Le Figaro (France), Der Spiegel (Germany), ANSA (Italy), NHK (Japan), NOS (Netherlands), NRK (Norway), SVT (Sweden), DR (Denmark), Yle (Finland), VnExpress (Vietnam), Antara (Indonesia).
- Google News RSS: used only when the reader chooses to focus the feed on a specific country, to retrieve that country's news in the reader's language.
- Apple on-device Text-to-Speech (AVSpeechSynthesis) for the read-aloud feature.
All settings, bookmarks, and reading history are stored on-device; nothing is sent to us.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app functions consistently in all regions — the same features are available everywhere with no geographic restrictions or region gating. The only differences are content-based and user-chosen: selecting a language or a country changes which sources and stories appear. A few minority-language options fall back to a national news outlet when a dedicated feed is unavailable. The app can be offered worldwide.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Newsphere is a news aggregator/reader, not part of a regulated industry, and requires no special credentials. It uses publicly available RSS feeds that publishers provide specifically for syndication. It shows headlines, short summaries, and thumbnail images, and links back to the original publisher's website for the full article (opened in an in-app browser) — it does not republish full article text and drives traffic to the source, consistent with standard RSS use. Source attribution is shown on every article.
